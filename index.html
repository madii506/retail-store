FAQ

Ask the cashier.

Why do I put a code in my X bio?

It proves the account is yours without any login, and it stops bots from emptying the giveaway. We only read your public profile: bio, follower and post counts, and account age. Delete the code once you're verified.

Who can claim?

Anyone 18+ with a public X account at least 30 days old, with 10+ followers and 5+ posts. The wallet must be new (fewer than 10 past transactions), because RETAIL is for first-timers.

How fast is the payout?

Payouts go out in the order people claimed, usually within minutes while the till is staffed. Some claims get a quick human check first, which can take up to 24 hours. Your ticket page shows your place in line and flips to PAID with the transaction link.

How much SOL is $20?

$20 worth of SOL at the price when your payout is sent. Right now that's about — SOL.

What if today's spots are gone?

A fixed number of spots (500) opens every day at 00:00 UTC. The live dashboard at the top shows what's left.

Do I have to buy $RETAIL?

No. Its creator fees pay for the giveaway. What you do with your $20 is up to you.

How do I know the payouts are real?

Every receipt links to its transaction on Solscan, every payout memo carries its receipt number, the payout wallet is public, and the site's code is open on GitHub.

Someone DMed me saying they're from RETAIL.

That's a scam. We never DM, never ask for your secret words, and never ask you to send anything first. Block and report.
